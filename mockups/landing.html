How it works
Five minutes to your first diagnosis.
Describe your stack, log an incident, and let Cascade connect the dots.
01

Describe your stack

Docker services, ports, dependencies, known quirks. Free-text Markdown. Cascade remembers everything.

02

Log an incident

Paste an error, describe a symptom, or write a short summary. Cascade timestamps and indexes it.

03

Get AI diagnosis

Cascade injects your stack context and past resolutions into the prompt. You get answers specific to your infra.

04

Record the fix

What actually worked? Tag it to the incident. Next time, Cascade surfaces your own resolution first.
